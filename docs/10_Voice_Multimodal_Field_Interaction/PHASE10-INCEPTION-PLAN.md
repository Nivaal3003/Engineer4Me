# Engineer4Me Phase 10 Inception Plan

**Phase:** 10 — Voice, multimodal field interaction, and controlled activation execution

Phase 10 continues under the **continue until intervention-required gate** model. Work proceeds in bounded batches and remains fail-closed at every permission, identity, transport, protected-content, provider, deployment, service-worker, and native-packaging boundary.

## Activation ladder

1. **Source-only contracts:** scripted or manual transcript and metadata-only image/document descriptors; privacy, provenance, intent proposal, and review contracts.
2. **Permission readiness:** capability and policy evidence without requesting a microphone or camera prompt.
3. **Controlled local capture:** explicit user-gesture activation and ephemeral raw media only after an intervention-required gate.
4. **Controlled processing:** accepted local or external transcription, OCR, or vision only after privacy, security, licensing, data-location, and quality evidence.
5. **Protected execution:** live authentication, bearer attachment, backend-authoritative access, entitlement, audit, approval, cancellation, and zero-retry controls.
6. **Deployment and device distribution:** supported hosting, browser/device/accessibility proof, history fallback, PWA/service-worker approval, and native-mobile packaging.

## Batch 379–390

This batch implements only level 0:

- strict field-interaction source and transcript contracts;
- inactive microphone and camera permission readiness;
- metadata-only multimodal descriptors;
- provenance, privacy, and memory-only retention contracts;
- query/command intent proposals with no automatic operation selection;
- explicit review and local-preview approval states that never authorize execution;
- scripted in-memory voice and multimodal fixtures;
- accessible protected-workspace readiness and preview presentation;
- isolated type checking, unit tests, deterministic builds, and Playwright discovery only.

## Persistent non-claims

No live capture, permission prompt, external AI, local AI model, authentication, bearer token, backend request, protected-content access, command execution, retry, persistence, production deployment, service worker, PWA cache, or native package is activated. Final engineering approval and operational authorization remain with the user or authorized organisation. Vendor neutrality, evidence, standards, proprietary-product, privacy, security, and accessibility controls remain mandatory.
